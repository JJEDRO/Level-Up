# 🏋️ 60-Day Gym Tracker Guide

## Overview

The 60-Day Gym Challenge Tracker helps you monitor your gym visits over a structured 60-day period. It tracks how many times you go to the gym and how many hours you spend working out, with the ability to export all data to Google Sheets for advanced analysis.

## Features

### 📊 Track Your Progress
- **Total Visits**: Count of gym sessions completed
- **Total Hours**: Sum of all workout duration 
- **Current Streak**: Consecutive days with gym visits
- **Days Remaining**: Countdown to complete your 60-day challenge

### 📅 Two View Modes
1. **Calendar View**: Visual 60-day calendar showing your gym visits
2. **List View**: Recent gym visits with details and notes

### 🏋️ Log Gym Visits
- **Date**: When you visited the gym
- **Duration**: How many hours you worked out (0.25 to 8 hours)
- **Notes**: What you focused on during your workout

### 📋 Google Sheets Export
Export your complete 60-day data as a CSV file for Google Sheets analysis.

## How to Use

### 1. Starting Your 60-Day Challenge
- The tracker automatically starts your 60-day challenge from today
- Your challenge end date is calculated as 60 days from the start date

### 2. Logging a Gym Visit
1. Click the **"🏋️ Log Gym Visit"** button
2. Select the date (defaults to today)
3. Enter duration in hours (e.g., 1.5 for 1 hour 30 minutes)
4. Add optional notes about your workout
5. Click **"Save Visit"**

### 3. Viewing Your Progress
- **Calendar View**: See all 60 days with colored indicators for gym visits
  - Green days = gym visits logged
  - Today is highlighted with an orange border
  - Click any past day to edit that visit
- **List View**: Recent visits displayed in chronological order
  - Click any visit to edit it

### 4. Exporting to Google Sheets
1. Click **"📋 Export to Google Sheets"**
2. A CSV file will automatically download
3. Follow the on-screen instructions:
   - Open Google Sheets (sheets.google.com)
   - Create a new spreadsheet
   - Click File → Import
   - Upload your downloaded CSV file
   - Choose "Replace current sheet"

## CSV Data Structure

Your exported data includes:
- **Date**: Full date for each of the 60 days
- **Day of Week**: Monday, Tuesday, etc.
- **Duration (Hours)**: Hours worked out (0 for non-gym days)
- **Notes**: Your workout notes or "No gym visit"
- **Week Number**: Week 1-9 for easy grouping

## Google Sheets Enhancement Ideas

Once in Google Sheets, you can:
- **Create Charts**: Visualize your workout frequency and duration trends
- **Conditional Formatting**: Highlight gym days in green
- **Weekly Summaries**: Use formulas to calculate weekly totals
- **Progress Goals**: Track toward targets (e.g., 3x per week)
- **Share with Friends**: Add accountability partners

## Data Storage

- All data is saved locally in your browser
- Data persists between sessions
- Export regularly as backup
- Each 60-day challenge is independent

## Tips for Success

1. **Be Consistent**: Try to log visits immediately after your workout
2. **Set Realistic Goals**: Start with 2-3 visits per week
3. **Track Progress Weekly**: Export and review your data weekly
4. **Use Notes**: Record what you worked on for better tracking
5. **Share Progress**: Export and share with workout partners

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Can I edit a past gym visit?**
A: Yes, click on any past day in calendar view or any visit in list view to edit.

**Q: What if I miss logging a visit?**
A: You can add visits for any past date within your 60-day period.

**Q: Can I start a new 60-day challenge?**
A: The tracker automatically uses your first logged visit as the start date for each independent session.

**Q: How accurate should my duration be?**
A: You can enter durations in 15-minute increments (0.25 hours). Round to the nearest quarter hour.
